Workforce analytics · Power BI · ML01

Employee Attrition Analytics System

An HR analytics case study using the IBM employee attrition sample. It combines data cleaning and exploration, model comparison, a four-page Power BI report, and a multi-page Streamlit prototype.

  • 1,470employees analysed
  • 30.5% vs 10.4%attrition with and without overtime
  • 35.9%recall on the attrition class, single holdout
Model results and interpretation

On a single 80/20 holdout, Logistic Regression achieved 89.46% accuracy and 35.9% recall for attrition; the saved Random Forest achieved 87.41% accuracy. The report also identifies a feature-schema mismatch in the app scoring path. The prototype requires correction and further validation before its predictions can be relied on. These group differences show association, not causation.

Market research · AI investment04

The AI Investment Boom: Bubble Risk & Market Structure

An evidence-led global market-risk report tracing links among AI investment, cloud and compute contracts, infrastructure commitments, and financing. It compares historical crash pathways and maps signals to monitor.

Scope and interpretation

The report treats an AI-linked correction as credible, while a wider financial crisis depends on credit, liquidity, and counterparty stress. It does not predict a crash date or recommend a security transaction; its scenarios and financial measures have distinct scopes.
