How I Approach a Problem

01 · INTERROGATE FIRST

02 · BUILD THE PIPELINE

WORKFORCE INTELLIGENCE / PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS

EAAS — Employee Attrition Analysis System

Built around a 1,470-record,35-feature IBM HR dataset,EAAS evaluates employee attrition through a comparative machine-learning workflow. Logistic Regression and Random Forest were engineered and benchmarked,culminating in 89.5% classification accuracy and real-time risk scoring with probability-confidence output. A Power BI executive dashboard covers attrition trends,salary bands and satisfaction patterns,alongside a multi-page Streamlit application handling ingestion,feature engineering and live prediction.
