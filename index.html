WORKFORCE INTELLIGENCE / PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS

EAAS — Employee Attrition Analysis System

Built around a 1,470-record, 35-feature IBM HR dataset, EAAS evaluates employee attrition through a comparative machine-learning workflow. Logistic Regression and Random Forest were engineered and benchmarked, culminating in 89.5% classification accuracy and real-time risk scoring with probability-confidence output.

The project didn't stop at model training: a Power BI executive dashboard covers attrition trends, salary bands and satisfaction patterns, alongside a multi-page Streamlit application handling ingestion, feature engineering and live prediction.

How I approach a problem

Rigour first. Insight second. Action always.

01

Interrogate first

Before choosing a technique, establish what the data can legitimately support: structure, quality, missingness, anomalies and constraints.

02

Build the pipeline
