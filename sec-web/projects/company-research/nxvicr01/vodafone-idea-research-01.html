An independent research note on Vodafone Idea's Q2 and H1 FY27 results, filed on 12 November 2026. Revenue and ARPU are growing and subscriber additions have resumed (the base is still 2.3m below last year), but negative net worth, large spectrum and AGR obligations and continuing funding needs keep this a high-risk turnaround, not a conventional cheap stock.

Investment view · 12 November 2026

The operating trend is better; the balance sheet remains the harder problem.

Q2 and H1 FY27 results were filed before the research cut-off. The share-price snapshot of ₹12.88 is from 11 November, before those results, so the operating update and valuation reference date are deliberately kept distinct.

01

Revenue and EBITDA are growing

Q2 revenue was ₹11,884 crore, +6.2% YoY and +1.7% QoQ. Operating EBITDA was ₹4,981 crore, +6.3% YoY but down 1.1% QoQ. The margin slipped 120 basis points QoQ to 41.9%.

02

ARPU and data use improve

Customer ARPU excluding M2M was ₹198, up 9.7% YoY on the company-reported basis; the rounded ₹180-to-₹198 comparison implies about 10.0%, with the difference attributable to rounded displayed values. Blended ARPU was ₹178. Daily data usage reached 93.6 PB, +27.1% YoY. The 4G/5G base was 131.0 million.

03

Subscriber quality is mixed

Subscribers rose 1.3 million QoQ to 194.4 million, but remained 2.3 million below Q2 FY26. The reported overall churn KPI rose to 4.0% from 3.8% in Q1; separately, management said prepaid net subscriber churn improved from 1.5 million to 0.6 million. The 4G/5G base is about 67.4% of total subscribers.

04

Reported losses remain large

Q2 finance cost was ₹5,351 crore and D&A ₹5,505 crore. Pre-exception PBT loss was ₹5,499 crore; reported PAT loss was ₹5,975 crore after ₹462 crore of exceptional items.

Thesis in one line: execution, customer quality and repeatable cash generation need to improve fast enough to support a heavily leveraged, negative-net-worth business.

Equity and exceptional items

Reported FY26 profit does not represent normalized earnings

FY26 reported PAT was +₹34,552 crore, but included net exceptional gains of ₹58,607 crore, including a ₹55,622 crore AGR remeasurement gain that is not cash. Pre-exception loss was ₹24,059 crore.

Net worth was -₹35,758 crore in FY26 and -₹44,298 crore in Q2 FY27. At 30 September, the results-note deferred payment total (₹157,532 crore) and the balance-sheet DPO lines (₹149,961 crore) differ by ₹7,571 crore, which the filing does not reconcile.

The January 2026 interim AGR relief froze ₹87,695 crore. The April 2026 final DoT reassessment gave nominal AGR of ₹64,046 crore, payable in instalments: at least ₹100 crore/year in FY32–35, then ₹10,608 crore/year in FY36–41. Present value ₹24,880 crore and carrying value ₹25,254 crore are separate bases; the ₹374 crore difference is unreconciled.

Pre-results valuation snapshot

Multiples depend on which cash path is used

At the 11 November pre-results price of ₹12.88, basic market capitalization was about ₹139,546 crore and enterprise value excluding leases about ₹296,151 crore.

EV / LTM cash EBITDA was 30.3x. EV / FY29 cash EBITDA was 10.7x on management's path and 19.6x on analyst Case B; these are directional and depend on very different future cash outcomes.

P/E and P/B are not meaningful with negative normalized earnings and negative book equity. Peer multiples are withheld where scope or ARPU definitions are not comparable.

Dilution and ownership

Capital structure can change per-share value

September 2026 ownership: promoters 25.64%, Government of India 49.00%, public excluding GOI 25.36%. There were 4.30 billion warrants against 108.343 billion issued shares; full exercise would raise shares to 112.643 billion, roughly 4% dilution.

The warrants, funding lines and government dues are material to the equity pathway and should be tracked alongside subscriber growth and capex execution.

Sections 04 and 16

Long-run trend, corrected

  • FY15–FY16: legacy Idea revenue rose to ₹35,949 crore in FY16.
  • FY17: revenue eased about 1.0% to ₹35,576 crore as pricing pressure intensified.
  • FY18: revenue fell 20.5% to ₹28,279 crore, EBITDA fell 41.0% to ₹6,048 crore, and margin dropped from 28.8% to 21.4%.
  • FY19–FY24: revenue recovered from the merger trough, but depreciation, finance cost and statutory commitments absorbed the gains.
  • FY25–FY26: FY26 revenue reached ₹44,873 crore. Reported PAT was positive only because of exceptional gains; the loss before exceptional items and tax was ₹24,059 crore.

Section 22

Integrated conclusion

Vodafone Idea is a high-risk, policy-sensitive turnaround under execution, not a conventional low-multiple value stock. The operating recovery is real enough to monitor, but it is not yet equivalent to sustainable equity cash generation.

The decisive proof points are repeated subscriber stabilisation, network improvement that can be monetised, definitive funding, cash conversion after capex and leases, manageable statutory payments, and limited value-destructive dilution.

Subscriber composition · management commentary

1.3m net additions were not broad-based prepaid growth

Management said the main drivers of Q2’s 1.3m sequential net additions were M2M and postpaid. Prepaid net subscriber churn improved from 1.5m in Q1 FY27 to 0.6m in Q2 FY27. That is a meaningful sequential improvement, but prepaid remained in net outflow.

Analytical implication: describe this as improving churn and mix, not a confirmed return to broad-based prepaid net additions.

Funding and execution · management commentary

Funding mobilisation is progressing, but not all funding is cash drawn

Management stated that more than ₹10,500 crore of funding had been secured through H1 FY27, including warrants and fund / non-fund based facilities, with more than ₹4,000 crore of incremental bank funding arranged in Q2. The company also reported Q2 capex of ₹3,946 crore and capex orders above ₹13,200 crore against its ₹45,000 crore three-year programme.

Analytical implication: distinguish secured facilities, non-fund limits, warrant proceeds, actual drawdowns, purchase orders and deployed network assets. They are not interchangeable.
