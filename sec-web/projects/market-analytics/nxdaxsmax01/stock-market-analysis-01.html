Project overview

What the project contains

A descriptive report on nine NSE tickers, built so each figure traces back to a filter, a date and a data snapshot.

The report combines daily market history with interactive filters, so a reader can review one company or move across the full ticker set without rebuilding any chart.

This page re-checks the Power BI dashboard against the supplied CSV files. Where the dashboard and the data disagree, the difference is shown in the tables rather than hidden.

Timeline check

The build date and the data window do not match

The project owner dates the build to 23 March 2024. The CSV filenames indicate a window from January 2025 to January 2026, and the rows run from 13 January 2025 to 9 January 2026, so the data matches its filenames. The supplied data looks like a later snapshot than the build date suggests.

Interactive filters
  • Company: selects the ticker to review.
  • Year: limits the view to a selected calendar year.
  • Quarter: narrows the selected year to a quarter.
  • Month: focuses the view on selected months.
  • Day: narrows the current selection to days.
Supporting visuals
  • Monthly 52-week high and low with volume: how the range indicators and trading volume vary by month.
  • Opening and closing price trend: how the selected ticker's prices move over time.
  • Monthly volume treemap: how the selected volume is distributed across months.

Open total does not match. The CSVs sum to 394,580.10 (394.58K) for OPEN, not 394.39K. The Open card shows the same value as the Close card, and the Close sum does match, so the Open measure may point at the close column. Check its definition in Power BI Desktop before presenting this card.

Tooltip check. The screenshot tooltip is dated 20 August 2025. The CSV row for 20-Aug-2025 shows open 1,468.00 and close 1,496.00, which matches the tooltip.

HCLTECH charts, recomputed from the CSV

Why the dashboard merges two Januaries. The month visuals group by month name and do not separate years, so one January bar adds Jan 2025 and Jan 2026 together. In the CSVs those two partial months are 67.6M + 17.4M shares, a combined 85.0M. Use a year-month key before comparing months.

Ticker comparison

Nine tickers on one page

Every chart below is recomputed from the CSVs and uses one colour for one measure. Compare shapes and percentages. Share counts differ by price level, so compare traded value in rupees where you need a like-for-like scale.

Read the change column with care. HDFCBANK's close falls from 1,964.10 to 973.40 on 26 Aug 2025 (-50.44%). Average daily volume over the 20 sessions from that date is 2.2 times the 20 sessions before it, and the 52-week high stays at 2,037.70 after the drop. ITC falls -9.71% on 1 Jan 2026 on 282.6M shares, about 34 times its prior 20-session average. Both patterns fit a corporate event, such as a bonus issue, split or demerger, but the files do not adjust prices for corporate actions. Confirm both events in NSE corporate-action records before reading these percentages as price performance.

Workflow and reproducibility

How to open and use the report

Steps
  1. Open the report. Open reports/Project_Stock.pbix in Power BI Desktop.
  2. Set the view. Use the Company, Year, Quarter, Month and Day slicers.
  3. Cross-check. Compare the KPI cards with the monthly and time-series charts for the same filter selection.
  4. Fix refresh if needed. If refresh asks for a source location, open Transform data and review the data source settings. Point the report to the project files under data/raw/stocks or data/reference, as appropriate.

Data notes and publication

Data-quality and cleaning notes

IssueWhy it mattersAction
Data window vs build dateFilenames show January 2025 to January 2026, later than the 23 March 2024 build date. Rows run 13 Jan 2025 to 9 Jan 2026.Record the snapshot date and do not imply the data was collected in 2024.
AIRTELPP formatTwo-digit years in DATE and series E1, while the other files use four-digit years and series EQ.Normalize DATE and SERIES before combining the files.
VALUE fieldStored as quoted text with Indian-style digit grouping.Remove the grouping commas carefully, then convert to a numeric type.
Open total on the dashboardRecomputed OPEN sum is 394,580.10; the card shows 394.39K, the same as the Close card.Check the Open measure definition in Power BI before presenting it.
Month visuals merge yearsJanuary combines Jan 2025 and Jan 2026, both partial months, into one bar.Use a year-month key for monthly visuals and mark partial months.
Summing 52-week rangesThe monthly chart sums 52-week highs and lows across days, which is hard to interpret as a range.Prefer averages, or minimum and maximum, for range indicators.
Prices not adjusted for corporate actionsHDFCBANK's close halves on 26 Aug 2025 with a volume step-up, and ITC drops 9.71% on 1 Jan 2026 on about 34 times normal volume. The 52-week high for HDFCBANK stays at 2,037.70 after the drop, so the range columns mix pre- and post-event prices.Confirm the events in NSE corporate-action records. Adjust prices before reading the change, best-day and volatility figures as performance.
README wordingThe README describes the screenshot's price trend as running from March 2025. The CSV series begins on 13 January 2025.Update the README wording to match the data.
Source attribution and reuseThe NSE attribution comes from the project owner. The exact download URL, retrieval date and redistribution terms are not in the archive, and no project license is included.Confirm attribution and reuse terms before publishing the raw market files.
